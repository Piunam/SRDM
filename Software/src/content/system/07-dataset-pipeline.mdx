### What it is

A generator that manufactures training pairs instead of collecting them. It follows the approach of
**MS-SNSD** and extends the data pipeline that ships with RNNoise: clean speech is mixed with noise
at a controlled signal-to-noise ratio, and both the mixture and the clean reference are kept, so
every example comes with its own ground truth.

Generating rather than recording is what makes the SNR axis controllable. The same utterance can be
emitted at −5, 0, +5 and +10 dB against the same gunshot, which is the only practical way to train a
model that has to behave sensibly across that whole range.

### Noise classes

- **Impulsive** — gunshots and artillery
- **Non-stationary** — helicopter rotors, armoured vehicles, sirens and drones
- **Stationary** — engine and generator hum, wind

### Augmentation

- Random mixing: which noise, how many layers, where it lands in the utterance
- Reverberation, to stand in for enclosed spaces and vehicle interiors
- Clipping, because a loud transient into a real preamp is rarely linear

<Why>
  Public speech-enhancement datasets are built around offices, cafés and
  streets. They contain almost no gunfire, no rotor wash and no armour, and
  impulsive events in particular are under-represented to the point of absence.
  A model trained on them learns the wrong prior for this job, so the pairs are
  generated here — at whatever scale the training run needs, with the noise
  classes that actually matter.
</Why>

<Callout tone="limit">
  Synthetic mixing is not a field recording. It does not reproduce microphone
  placement, body shielding, wind against the grille or the acoustics of a real
  vehicle. Field evaluation is on the [roadmap](#roadmap) precisely because this
  pipeline cannot answer that question.
</Callout>
