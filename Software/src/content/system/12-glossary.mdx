    ["SNR", "Signal-to-noise ratio. How much louder the speech is than the noise, in decibels. 0 dB means equally loud; negative means the noise is louder."],

    ["GRU", "Gated recurrent unit. A recurrent network cell that keeps a hidden state between frames, so the model's decision about this frame depends on what it heard in the last few hundred milliseconds."],
    ["QAT", "Quantisation-aware training. Training with the deployment precision — here int8 — simulated in the forward pass, so the model learns weights that survive being rounded down from float."],
    ["Bark bands", "A frequency grouping that follows the ear's resolution: narrow at low frequencies, wide at high ones. Predicting one gain per band instead of per bin is what keeps the model small."],
    ["Comb filter", "A filter with regularly spaced notches. Locked to the speaker's pitch, it passes the voice's harmonics and attenuates the noise sitting between them."],
    ["Impulsive noise", "Noise that arrives as a burst: a gunshot, artillery, a door slam. It changes the spectrum faster than an adaptive filter can follow, which is why it needs the network."],
    ["Stationary noise", "Noise whose spectrum is roughly constant: engine hum, fans, wind. Predictable, and therefore cheap to remove with classical DSP."],
