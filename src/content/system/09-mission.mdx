Reliable, intelligible voice communication for people working in the loudest places on earth, on
hardware small enough to wear.

Noise is treated as a safety problem rather than an audio one. A transmission that has to be repeated
costs seconds, and the situations this device is built for are the situations where seconds are
expensive. The target is not a pleasant-sounding result; it is a result the listener does not have to
ask about twice.

### Who it's for

- **Defence** — dismounted and vehicle crews, rotary-wing aircrew, range and training staff
- **Aerospace** — flight line, ground support and engine test
- **High-noise industry** — mining, drilling, heavy plant, emergency response
